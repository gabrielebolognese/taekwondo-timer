# Taekwondo Training Plan

Oct 15, 2026 · @Gabriele Bolognese

## Rules

6 months, 6 training days a week. Months 1 to 3 move fast (form fixes), months 4 to 6 move slower.

| Day | Session |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Power A |
| Tue | Flexibility + kicks |
| Wed | Tricking form + target |
| Thu | Power B |
| Fri | Flexibility + kicks |
| Sat | Max tricking + test |
| Sun | GTG only + 10 min slow kicks |

- **Week 4 of every month:** deload. Do half the sets, same effort.
- **Stop rule:** 3 reps in a row under 90% of your session best (height, flight time or rotation) = that exercise is done for the day.
- **Rest:** jumps and strength 2 min, contrast rounds 3 min, tricks 60 to 90 s, flexibility none.
- **Ball height:** start at the height you hit 8/10. 8/10 = raise it 5 cm next session. Under 5/10 = lower it 5 cm.
- **Film** every trick set on Wed and Sat.

**Warm-ups**

- **Power (Mon, Thu):** 3 min jog, pogos 3×20, leg swings 15 per direction per leg, arm swing jumps 4×3.
- **Flexibility (Tue, Fri):** 5 min jog or jump rope, leg swings 15 per direction per leg.
- **Tricking (Wed, Sat):** 5 min jog, leg swings 15 per direction, 10 easy kicks per side, 5 360 tuck jumps.

**Hip block (Mon, Thu):** hanging knee raise to tight tuck, lying straight-leg raise above 90° (2 s hold), side-lying abduction, Copenhagen plank, disc woodchop. Sets and load are in each month.
