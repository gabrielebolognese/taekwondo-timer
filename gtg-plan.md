# GTG Daily Plan

Oct 15, 2026 · @Gabriele Bolognese

## Rules

6 to 8 micro-sets a day, every day, each 2 to 3 min.

- At least 1 hour between sets.
- Effort 50 to 70%. Never to failure, never a max stretch.
- Rotate A, B, C, D through the day.
- Mon and Thu: skip set D (legs are fresh for power).
- 1 hour before and after the main session: no GTG.
- Week 4 of every month: 4 sets a day instead of 6 to 8.

## Sets

Each set changes every 2 months.

| Set | Months 1 to 2 | Months 3 to 4 | Months 5 to 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: front | Front split hold 30 s per side, then 5 front leg raise holds per leg (3 s) | Front split hold 45 s per side, then 5 front leg raise holds per leg (3 s) | Front split hold 60 s per side, then 5 front leg raise holds per leg (5 s) |
| B: side | Side split or horse stance 30 s, then 5 side leg raise holds per leg (3 s) | Side split 45 s, then 5 side leg raise holds per leg (3 s) | Side split 60 s, then 5 side leg raise holds per leg (5 s) |
| C: kicks | 5 slow chambered kicks per side to a chest-height wall mark, 1 s freeze | 5 per side to chest mark, 2 s freeze | 5 per side to a mark 5 cm above chest, 2 s freeze |
| D: spins | 5 360 tuck jumps at 70%, soft surface | 5 540 tuck jumps at 70%, soft surface | 3 720 spin drills at 70%, on mat |
